about the series

It all started with chaos. In the Programming Guide you flee a city in ruins, find a group of survivors, manage to hold out in an abandoned power plant, and after a hasty escape end up defending a prison through its first winter. Along the way you'll learn TypeScript from scratch, which isn't the first thing anyone would do in an apocalypse, but here that's the job.

The volumes that follow change the threat. In Domain-Driven Design the danger is no longer just the horde but a shelter that keeps growing until nobody understands how it runs anymore. And in AI-Assisted Development the Awakened show up: machines that write code faster than you can read it, and someone will have to decide what they're allowed to do, and how much.

You can read each volume on its own, but they all share the setting, the characters, and a small story that unfolds little by little. The structure is similar too: first a situation comes up, then comes the code, and in between the Survivor's Tips and the Notes from the Cabin come to the rescue with advice and answers to the most common questions. The Spanish editions come out first. The English ones follow as they get translated, and they show up in this same archive.

Pick a volume, check its threat level, and if you like what you see, grab it before the end of the world gets here.
